5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone-proguanil, doxycycline, or tafenoquine

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return

   • How to Take: Take daily with food

   • Common Side Effects: Nausea, diarrhea, headache

   • Additional Protection: Use mosquito repellent, wear long sleeves and pants, sleep under a mosquito net

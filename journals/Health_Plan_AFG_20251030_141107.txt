5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone/proguanil, Doxycycline, or Mefloquine. Consult with a travel health specialist to determine the best option.

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel (Atovaquone/proguanil), 1-2 days before travel (Doxycycline), 2-3 weeks before travel (Mefloquine)
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return (Atovaquone/proguanil and Doxycycline), 4 weeks after return (Mefloquine)

   • How to Take: Take daily with food.

   • Common Side Effects: Vary depending on the medication. Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, and headache.

   • Additional Protection: Use mosquito repellent with DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, and sleep in screened or air-conditioned rooms or under a mosquito net.

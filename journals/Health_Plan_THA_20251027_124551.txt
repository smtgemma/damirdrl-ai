6. Essential Health Precautions

**Food & Water Safety**
Practice safe food and water habits. Eat only thoroughly cooked food, drink only bottled or boiled water, and avoid ice.

**Insect Bite Prevention**
Protect yourself from mosquito bites to prevent diseases like dengue fever, Zika virus, and Japanese encephalitis. Use insect repellent containing DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, and sleep in air-conditioned or screened rooms.

**Travel Insurance**
Ensure you have comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical emergencies, evacuation, and repatriation. Verify that the insurance covers activities planned during the trip.

**When to Seek Medical Help**
Seek medical attention if you develop a fever, rash, persistent diarrhea, or other concerning symptoms during or after your trip.

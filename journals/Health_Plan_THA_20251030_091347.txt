4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: Almost 100% after two doses
   • Immunity duration: At least 25 years with two doses
   • Booster info: Not generally needed after two doses


Hepatitis B:
   • Protection starts: After the second dose
   • Full protection: After completing the series
   • Immunity duration: Long-term, possibly lifelong
   • Booster info: Not usually needed, but may be recommended for certain individuals


Typhoid (Injection):
   • Protection starts: 1-2 weeks after vaccination
   • Full protection: Within 2 weeks
   • Immunity duration: Approximately 2 years
   • Booster info: Recommended every 2 years for continued risk


Typhoid (Oral Capsules):
   • Protection starts: 1 week after the last capsule
   • Full protection: Within 1 week after completing all doses
   • Immunity duration: Approximately 5 years
   • Booster info: Recommended every 5 years for continued risk


Japanese Encephalitis:
   • Protection starts: Sufficient protective antibodies in 30% of vaccine recipients 10 days after vaccination
   • Full protection: More than 95% of vaccine recipients after the second dose
   • Immunity duration: Uncertain beyond three years after the first booster
   • Booster info: A single booster dose of IXIARO® booster 12 months after primary immunisation is recommended. Others should be offered a first booster dose at 12-24 months following the primary course, prior to re-exposure to JE virus. A 2nd booster (4th dose) should be offered at 10 years for those who remain at risk.


5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone-proguanil, Doxycycline, or Tafenoquine. Consult with a travel health specialist on which antimalarial will best fit your needs.

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return

   • How to Take: Follow specific instructions for chosen medication

   • Common Side Effects: Vary depending on medication; consult with a healthcare provider

   • Additional Protection: Use mosquito repellent with DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, sleep in air-conditioned or screened rooms, or under a mosquito net.

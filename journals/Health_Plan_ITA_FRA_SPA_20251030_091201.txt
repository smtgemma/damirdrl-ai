Typhoid:

   Injection:
   • Dose 1: At least 2 weeks before travel

   Oral Capsules:
   • Dose 1: Take 1 capsule every other day for 4 doses, complete at least 1 week before travel

   ⚠️  Not enough time to complete the oral Typhoid vaccine schedule before departure. Consult with a travel clinic.

4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: Almost 100% after two doses
   • Immunity duration: At least 20 years with booster
   • Booster info: A booster is recommended


Hepatitis B:
   • Protection starts: After the second dose
   • Full protection: 1-2 months after the third dose
   • Immunity duration: Long-lasting
   • Booster info: Check antibody levels if at high risk


Typhoid:
   • Protection starts: 1-3 weeks after vaccination
   • Full protection: Varies depending on the vaccine type
   • Immunity duration: Injection: 2 years; Oral: 5 years
   • Booster info: Booster doses are recommended


Measles-Mumps-Rubella (MMR):
   • Protection starts: 2 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: 2 weeks after second dose
   • Immunity duration: Lifelong
   • Booster info: Generally considered lifelong, but revaccination may be recommended during outbreaks


Tetanus-Diphtheria-Pertussis (Tdap):
   • Protection starts: 2 weeks after vaccination
   • Full protection: 2 weeks after vaccination
   • Immunity duration: 10 years
   • Booster info: Booster recommended every 10 years


Influenza:
   • Protection starts: 2 weeks after vaccination
   • Full protection: 2 weeks after vaccination
   • Immunity duration: About 6 months to a year
   • Booster info: Annual vaccination recommended


Polio:
   • Protection starts: After completion of the series
   • Full protection: After completion of the series
   • Immunity duration: Lifelong
   • Booster info: A single lifetime booster dose is recommended for adults traveling to certain areas.

3. Vaccination Schedule Plan


Hepatitis A:
   • Dose 1: Today or as soon as possible
   • Dose 2: 6 months after Dose 1
     Note: Dose 2 can be administered after travel.


Hepatitis B:

   Standard Schedule:
   • Dose 1: Today or as soon as possible
   • Dose 2: 1 month after Dose 1
   • Dose 3: 6 months after Dose 1

   Accelerated Schedule:
   • Dose 1: Today or as soon as possible
   • Dose 2: 7 days after Dose 1
   • Dose 3: 21 days after Dose 1
   • Dose 4: 12 months after Dose 1
     Note: This is a common choice of vaccine for those traveling on short-notice. It is important to complete the booster dose at 1 year, to ensure long-term protection.


Typhoid:

   Injection:
   • Dose 1: At least 2 weeks before travel
     Note: Booster recommended every 2 years for continued risk.

   Oral Capsules:
   • Dose 1: Take 1 capsule on alternate days, completing all 4 capsules at least 1 week before potential exposure.
     Note: Must be able to swallow pills. Oral doses must be kept in refrigerator. Booster dose of Ty21a should be taken every 5 years, if indicated.


Japanese Encephalitis:

   Standard Schedule:
   • Dose 1: Today or as soon as possible
   • Dose 2: 28 days after Dose 1
     Note: The last dose of IXIARO should be administered at least 1 week before travel.

   Accelerated Schedule:
   • Dose 1: Today or as soon as possible
   • Dose 2: 7 days after Dose 1
     Note: The last dose of IXIARO should be administered at least 1 week before travel. Contact clinic to see if accelerated schedule is appropriate.

   ⚠️  Warning: Dose 2 falls between 15 Nov 2025 and 15 Dec 2025. Contact clinic to see if accelerated schedule is appropriate.


4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: 2 weeks after second dose
   • Immunity duration: At least 20 years with booster
   • Booster info: A booster is recommended at least 6 months after the first dose.


Hepatitis B:
   • Protection starts: After the second dose
   • Full protection: After the third dose
   • Immunity duration: Lifetime
   • Booster info: If an alternative schedule is considered, ensure that a 4th booster dose is given at 1 year to provide maximum, long term protection.


Typhoid:
   • Protection starts: 1-3 weeks after vaccination
   • Full protection: 1-3 weeks after vaccination
   • Immunity duration: 2 years (injection), 5 years (oral)
   • Booster info: Boosters are recommended every 2 years for the injectable form and every 5 years for the oral form.


Japanese Encephalitis:
   • Protection starts: Unknown
   • Full protection: At least one week after the second dose
   • Immunity duration: At least 12 months, booster recommended after 12-24 months
   • Booster info: A booster dose should be considered after 12-24 months if ongoing risk.


5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone-proguanil, Doxycycline, or Tafenoquine

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return

   • How to Take: Take daily as directed by your healthcare provider.

   • Common Side Effects: Consult your doctor or pharmacist for potential side effects.

   • Additional Protection: Use mosquito repellent with DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, and sleep in screened or air-conditioned rooms.

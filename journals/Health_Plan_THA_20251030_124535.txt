4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: Almost 100% after two doses
   • Immunity duration: At least 20 years with booster
   • Booster info: Booster recommended


Typhoid:
   • Protection starts: 1-3 weeks after injection; 1 week after oral
   • Full protection: Varies by vaccine type
   • Immunity duration: 2 years (injection), 5 years (oral)
   • Booster info: Recommended every 2 years for injection, every 5 years for oral if risk continues


Hepatitis B:
   • Protection starts: After the 2nd dose
   • Full protection: After completion of the series
   • Immunity duration: Long-term, but may require booster in some individuals
   • Booster info: Check antibody levels to determine need for booster


Japanese Encephalitis:
   • Protection starts: 10 days after first dose
   • Full protection: 28 days after the first dose
   • Immunity duration: Uncertain beyond 3 years after first booster
   • Booster info: Single booster 12 months after primary immunization is recommended for ongoing risk. A 2nd booster (4th dose) should be offered at 10 years for those who remain at risk.


5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone/proguanil or doxycycline

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return

   • How to Take: Take daily

   • Common Side Effects: Varies by medication; consult doctor

   • Additional Protection: Mosquito bite prevention (DEET repellent, long sleeves/pants, bed net)

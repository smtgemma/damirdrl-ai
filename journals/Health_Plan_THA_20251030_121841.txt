4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: After two doses
   • Immunity duration: At least 25 years with two doses
   • Booster info: None needed after two doses


Typhoid:
   • Protection starts: 1-2 weeks after injection/oral capsule series
   • Full protection: 1-2 weeks after injection/oral capsule series
   • Immunity duration: 2 years (injection), 5 years (oral)
   • Booster info: Repeat dose every 2 years (injection) or repeat series every 5 years (oral) if risk continues


Japanese Encephalitis:
   • Protection starts: Sufficient protective antibodies in 30% of vaccine recipients 10 days after vaccination and in 40% of vaccine recipients 28 days post-vaccination
   • Full protection: A second dose of vaccine given 28 days after the first dose induces antibodies in more than 95% of vaccine recipients.
   • Immunity duration: Uncertain beyond three years after the first booster
   • Booster info: A single dose of IXIARO® booster 12 months after primary immunisation is recommended. Others should be offered the first booster dose at 12-24 months following the primary course, prior to re-exposure to JE virus. A 2nd booster (4th dose) should be offered at 10 years for those who remain at risk.


5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone-proguanil, Doxycycline, or Tafenoquine

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return

   • How to Take: Take daily as directed by your doctor

   • Common Side Effects: Varies by medication; discuss with your doctor

   • Additional Protection: Use mosquito repellent with DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, sleep in air-conditioned or screened rooms, or under insecticide-treated bed nets.

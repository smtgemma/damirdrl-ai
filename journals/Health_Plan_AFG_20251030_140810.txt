4. Vaccine Protection Timeline


Typhoid:
   • Protection starts: 1-3 weeks after injection; 1 week after oral capsule series
   • Full protection: 1-3 weeks after injection; 1 week after oral capsule series
   • Immunity duration: 2 years (injection); 5 years (oral)
   • Booster info: Booster every 2 years for injection, every 5 years for oral


Hepatitis A:
   • Protection starts: 2-4 weeks after first dose
   • Full protection: Almost 100% after 2 doses
   • Immunity duration: At least 20 years with booster
   • Booster info: Second dose recommended 6-12 months after first dose for long-term protection


Rabies:
   • Protection starts: 7 days after the 3rd dose
   • Full protection: 7 days after the 3rd dose
   • Immunity duration: Varies; booster may be needed for continued exposure
   • Booster info: Consult with healthcare provider regarding booster requirements based on ongoing risk.


Polio:
   • Protection starts: Unknown
   • Full protection: After booster dose
   • Immunity duration: Lifetime
   • Booster info: Single lifetime booster recommended for adults previously fully vaccinated as children.


5. Malaria Prevention Protocol

   Medication: Atovaquone/proguanil, doxycycline, or mefloquine. Consult with a travel health specialist to determine the best option.

   • When to Start: 1-2 days before travel (atovaquone/proguanil), 1-2 days before travel (doxycycline), 2-3 weeks before travel (mefloquine)
   • When to Stop: 7 days after return (atovaquone/proguanil and doxycycline), 4 weeks after return (mefloquine)

   • How to Take: Follow specific instructions provided by your healthcare provider and the medication label.

   • Common Side Effects: Varies depending on the medication. Common side effects can include nausea, diarrhea, headache, and dizziness.

   • Additional Protection: Use insect repellent with DEET, wear long sleeves and pants, and sleep in an air-conditioned or screened room or under an insecticide-treated bed net.
